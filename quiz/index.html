A LITTLE PRACTICE

Get to know your world.

Click a country or state, type its name and save. Answered places turn ochre. Grade checks only saved answers: fern green for correct and dusty rose for incorrect. The actual names appear on answered places; all others stay blank. Editing an answer clears grading for that map so you can try again.

Choose Show All to display every name, or Click to Show to reveal a name when you select a place. Choose Quiz to return to entering answers. Zoom in to separate nearby labels.

Capitalization, accents, spaces and punctuation do not affect grading. Common alternative country names are accepted. Country and state answers are saved separately in this browser, independently of your travel map.

Map and Globe share the 195-country quiz. States covers the 50 US states, with Alaska and Hawaii as insets. Natural Earth boundaries follow de facto conventions; disputed borders and recognition vary.

Drag to pan or rotate. Scroll or + / − to zoom. Home resets, / opens search in Show All, Escape closes panels. Tab and Enter can select map shapes. Tiny countries can also be selected from the numbered list.
