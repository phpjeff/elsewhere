A LITTLE PERSPECTIVE

A world of possibilities.

The travel checklist includes 193 United Nations member states plus Palestine and the Holy See: 195 countries. Territories and other land areas can also be selected, marked visited, and assigned categories. Their visits are counted separately from the 195 countries. Only areas represented in the map data are selectable.

Boundaries use Natural Earth’s 1:50m cartography and its de facto boundary conventions. Disputed borders and country recognition vary; this map does not express a political position. The map loads the latest available Natural Earth source when opened; cartographic releases may lag real-world changes.

Explore: drag or swipe to pan the map or rotate the globe; scroll, pinch, or + / − to zoom; arrow keys to pan; Home to reset; / to search; Escape to close panels. Search provides access to very small countries.

Your visits are saved in this browser only. Clearing browser data removes them.
